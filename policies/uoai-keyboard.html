UOAI Keyboard Privacy Policy

100% Offline Keyboard • Zero Keystroke Logging • Zero Ads • Zero Cloud APIs

UOAI Keyboard is built on a strict privacy-by-architecture guarantee: your keystrokes, typed text, drafts, clipboard items, templates, voice input, and AI prompts never leave your physical device. We operate no cloud servers, make zero external internet calls, require no account login, and bundle zero advertising (AD_ID / AdMob) or analytics SDKs.

1. Purpose of UOAI Keyboard

UOAI Keyboard is a fast, modern, ad-free Android Input Method Editor (IME) offering multilingual layouts, curated themes, mechanical switch sounds, clipboard history, quick text templates, offline voice typing, and an optional on-device AI Writing Assistant powered by the companion Universal Offline AI Provider (com.uoai.provider).

2. Keystroke, Incognito & Password Field Protection

  • Zero Remote Keystroke Transmission: Nothing you type is ever transmitted over the internet, uploaded to a cloud service, or shared with any third party.
  • Automatic Password Field Protection: When an active text field is marked by Android as a password, PIN, or sensitive input field, UOAI Keyboard automatically pauses word learning, next-word suggestions, clipboard recording, and AI writing features.
  • Incognito Mode: When an app requests incognito input (e.g., private browser tabs) or when you enable incognito mode, learning and history tracking are automatically paused.

3. On-Device AI Writing Assistant (Loopback Only)

When you explicitly invoke one of the 13 writing or reply modes (Grammar Fix, Polish & Clarity, Humanize, Enhance Prompt, Professional Email, Formal, Polite, Casual, Direct, Shorter, Bullets, Summary, or Smart Reply):

  • Strict Localhost Loopback IPC: UOAI Keyboard communicates exclusively with your local Universal Offline AI Provider (com.uoai.provider) over Android's loopback interface (http://127.0.0.1:8765). Cleartext loopback access is restricted strictly to 127.0.0.1 and localhost via uoai_network_security_config.xml—no external servers are ever contacted.
  • One-Time OS-Verified Approval: Access to the local provider requires your explicit one-time approval. The issued access token is encrypted in the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • No Remote AI Processing: Selected text and generated rewrites are processed entirely on your phone's local hardware using Gemma 3 / Gemma 4 models hosted by UOAI Provider.

4. Local Data Stored on Your Device

All keyboard features store their state strictly inside the app's private on-device storage sandbox:

  • Clipboard History: Copied text items are kept locally on your device so you can paste or pin them quickly. You can clear clipboard items at any time.
  • Quick Text Templates & Personal Dictionary: User-created templates, learned words, and language preferences stay strictly on-device.
  • App Preferences: Selected theme, mechanical sound/haptic profile, active keyboard layouts, and your last-used AI writing tone are stored locally.
  • Local JSON Backup & Restore: Exporting or importing templates and preferences creates/reads a local JSON file solely at the location you choose via Android's system file picker.
  • Offline Voice Typing: Audio captured when you explicitly tap the microphone button is processed locally on-device for speech-to-text input and is never stored as audio recordings or sent to our servers.

5. Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

UOAI Keyboard requests only the minimal permissions needed for offline keyboard functionality:

Permission Type Justification & Privacy Boundary
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Optional Runtime Offline Voice Typing: Requested only when you tap the microphone key to dictate text. Audio is never saved or transmitted to external servers. All typing and AI features work normally if denied.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Tactile Haptic Feedback: Provides customizable keypress vibration feedback while typing.
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Localhost Loopback IPC Only: Required by Android to open local TCP sockets to 127.0.0.1:8765 to communicate with com.uoai.provider on the same device (enforced via uoai_network_security_config.xml). Zero external servers are contacted.

6. Zero Ads, Zero Analytics & Zero Third-Party Sharing

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: Completely ad-free. AD_ID and AdMob SDKs are completely removed from the application.
  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry SDKs: No Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, or behavioral trackers.
  • Zero Crash Telemetry SDKs: No third-party crash reporting SDKs (such as Crashlytics or Sentry).
  • Zero Data Sharing or Profiling: We never collect, sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

8. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Because all data resides exclusively in your device's private application storage, you have complete control at all times:

  1. In-App Controls: Clear clipboard history, delete saved quick text templates, or reset preferences directly within UOAI Keyboard settings.
  2. Revoke AI Provider Access: Revoke UOAI Keyboard's local loopback token anytime inside UOAI Provider → Clients.
  3. Full Data Wipe: Go to Settings → Apps → UOAI Keyboard → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  4. Uninstallation: Uninstalling UOAI Keyboard permanently deletes all private app data, dictionary entries, templates, and Keystore tokens from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

UOAI Keyboard is rated for Everyone and does not collect, transmit, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because all processing is strictly offline and on-device, it complies with COPPA and GDPR.

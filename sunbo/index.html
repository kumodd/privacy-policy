100% Offline • Zero Cloud Servers • Zero Ads • Zero Tracking • AES-256-GCM Encrypted

Sunbo — Your 2nd Mind is built on a strict local-only architecture: your voice recordings, transcripts, extracted memories, tasks, citations, and AI queries never leave your own Android hardware. Sunbo requires no user account, no cloud subscription, and no API keys; operates zero remote backend servers; and bundles zero advertising (AD_ID), analytics, or crash-reporting telemetry SDKs.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection or Cloud Sharing

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, profile, or transmit any personal information, audio recordings, transcripts, memories, tasks, contacts, location data, device identifiers, or usage analytics to our servers or any third party.

  • No Accounts or Cloud Subscriptions: Sunbo works completely offline without login, registration, cloud sync accounts, or third-party AI API keys.
  • Zero Advertising & Telemetry SDKs: The app is free and ad-free, and includes no advertising SDKs, no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics, and no third-party tracking libraries.
  • No Cloud Backups: Android cloud backup is explicitly disabled in the application manifest (android:allowBackup="false") so private audio files, transcripts, memories, and pairing keys are never uploaded to cloud backup services.

2. Unified Dual-Mode Architecture & On-Device Processing

One unified app (com.personalai.primary) supports two reversible modes on your own Android devices, with safe in-app role switching that never uploads data to external servers:

Primary Mode (Sunbo — Your 2nd Mind)

  • Encrypted Bluetooth Reception: Receives encrypted speech segments over peer-to-peer Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE GATT / L2CAP CoC) directly from your own paired Secondary device, and also supports manual quick notes via + Capture.
  • 100% On-Device Speech Transcription (ASR): Transcribes English, Hindi, Hinglish, and multilingual speech strictly on-device using your selected local backend: Direct Gemma 4 Multimodal ASR, on-device Whisper (whisper.cpp), or on-device Hinglish Cactus via the optional local companion Universal Offline AI Provider (com.uoai.provider); a user-configured local loopback Whisper server (127.0.0.1 / localhost only); or built-in Android Offline Speech Recognition.
  • Local Memory Vault, Tasks & Cited Q&A: Organizes transcripts into 13 structured categories (Decisions, Commitments, Tasks, Deadlines, Ideas, Facts, Projects, Follow-Ups, and more), builds local semantic search indexes, calculates 7-day Insights & Digests, and streams grounded Ask Sunbo Q&A responses with clickable transcript citations ([S1]) entirely on your phone. A built-in zero-network rule engine is also included when no external local LLM provider is installed.

Secondary Mode (Hands-Free Encrypted Recorder Companion)

  • Silero VAD On-Device Speech Capture: Turns a spare Android phone or tablet (no SIM card or Wi-Fi required) into a dedicated voice recorder that runs on-device Silero Voice Activity Detection (VAD) to skip silence and capture speech segments.
  • Explicit Foreground Start & Visible Indicators: Recording starts strictly from an explicit foreground tap by you and always displays a persistent system notification with live recording state and an immediate Stop button alongside Android's system microphone indicator.
  • Privacy Kill-Switch & Safe Reboot Behavior: An instant in-app Privacy Kill-Switch forces the recorder to DISABLED and blocks arming or remote capture controls. After a device reboot or app update, the microphone always stays OFF until you explicitly open the app and arm capture.

3. Hardware-Grade Encryption, Pairing & Loopback Security

  • Mutual ECDH P-256 + 6-Digit SAS Pairing: Primary and Secondary devices authenticate using Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH P-256) key exchange verified by a mutual 6-digit Short Authentication String (SAS) and Android Bluetooth bonding.
  • End-to-End Encrypted Bluetooth Transfer: Every BLE L2CAP CoC audio chunk transmitted between your paired devices is encrypted in transit with AES-256-GCM using ephemeral ECDH session keys and verified with SHA-256 checksums before acknowledgement (ACK).
  • Hardware-Backed Local Encryption at Rest: Captured WAV segments and pairing credentials are protected using AES-256-GCM keys managed by the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • Loopback-Only Network Lock: The INTERNET permission is restricted by network_security_config.xml and an in-app host allowlist (which throws a SecurityException for any cloud or LAN host) strictly to 127.0.0.1 and localhost for local IPC with com.uoai.provider (127.0.0.1:8765) or a local loopback Whisper service. No packets ever leave your device over the internet.

6. Local Storage, Smart Audio Auto-Deletion & User Controls

All app data is stored exclusively inside Sunbo's private on-device storage sandbox (allowBackup="false"):

  • Encrypted Audio Segments: Captured and synced speech WAV clips stored locally with Android Keystore AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Local Memory Database: Transcripts, structured memories (13 categories), tasks, reminders, entities, daily/weekly summaries, local semantic embeddings, and Ask Sunbo Q&A history stored in an on-device Room SQLite database.
  • Preferences & Pairing Credentials: App mode, ASR/LLM settings, retention policy, and Keystore-protected ECDH peer credentials stored locally via Jetpack DataStore and Android Keystore.
  • Smart Audio Auto-Deletion: You can automatically reclaim storage by configuring verified WAV deletion After ASR, after 1h / 6h / 12h, or after 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 days (or Keep All), while preserving 100% of transcripts, memories, and tasks on-device. Pending or failed clips are never auto-deleted.
  • User-Initiated Exports: WAV files are written to public Downloads/Sunbo/ only when you explicitly tap Export on a clip.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Per-Item Deletion: Delete individual audio clips, transcripts, memories, or tasks directly inside Sunbo.
  2. One-Tap Full Wipe: Tap Delete All Local Audio, Transcripts & Memories in Settings to erase all local recordings, transcripts, memories, embeddings, tasks, digests, and Q&A history.
  3. Unpair Devices & Kill-Switch: Forget paired devices in Settings to purge stored ECDH keys, or toggle the Secondary Mode Privacy Kill-Switch at any time.
  4. Optional Companion Revocation: If connected to Universal Offline AI Provider (com.uoai.provider), you can revoke Sunbo's local loopback token anytime inside UOAI Provider (see the UOAI Provider Privacy Policy).
  5. Android System Wipe / Uninstall: Use Android Settings → Apps → Sunbo → Storage → Clear Storage or uninstall the app to permanently remove all private app data and Keystore keys. Any clips you manually exported to Downloads/Sunbo/ remain in your device's Downloads folder until you delete them.

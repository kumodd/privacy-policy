100% On-Device • Zero Cloud • Zero Tracking • Zero Accounts

The Universal Offline AI (UOAI) ecosystem was designed from the ground up on a strict privacy-first principle: your data, prompts, documents, and conversations never leave your physical device. We run open-source large language models (such as Gemma) locally on your device's hardware (CPU, GPU, NPU) through an on-device loopback server. We do not operate remote inference servers, we do not require account logins, and we do not bundle any analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. Applications Covered by This Policy

This privacy policy applies to the following two independently published applications available on Google Play:

UOAI Provider
com.uoai.provider

Acts as the local, shared on-device inference hub. Hosts local Gemma models and exposes an authenticated loopback HTTP/SSE server strictly on 127.0.0.1:8765 for approved Android client applications.

UOAI Chat
com.uoai.chat

A standalone conversational client application. Connects to UOAI Provider over loopback, extracts text locally from user-selected PDFs, documents, or code, and displays private streaming answers.

2. UOAI Provider Privacy Details

2.1 What UOAI Provider Stores Locally

  • Downloaded AI Model Files: Local GGUF, LiteRT-LM (.litertlm), or MediaPipe (.task) model files stored in private app storage. These files are downloaded directly from open-source repositories (such as Hugging Face) over direct HTTPS connections initiated by the user, or imported from your local storage.
  • Approved Client App Registrations: The package name and SHA-256 signing certificate fingerprint of client apps you explicitly authorize via Android OS Activity results.
  • Cryptographic Access Tokens: Locally generated tokens stored in the secure Android Keystore, used solely to authenticate loopback requests from approved apps on the same device.
  • Inference Audit Trail (Metadata Only): Optional local logs showing timestamps, caller package IDs, token counts, and latency. Prompt content and response text are never recorded or stored in audit logs.

2.2 Loopback-Only Network Architecture

The embedded HTTP/SSE server (powered by Ktor CIO) binds strictly to 127.0.0.1:8765 (loopback interface). It is never bound to 0.0.0.0, never listens on Wi-Fi or cellular interfaces, and cannot be accessed by external computers, servers, or devices on your local network.

3. UOAI Chat Privacy Details

3.1 Chat History & Conversations

All conversation messages, turns, and assistant outputs are kept strictly in local memory and private app state. We do not upload your chat sessions to any cloud server, database, or telemetry platform. Clearing the chat within the app permanently frees the conversational memory.

3.2 Local Document & File Attachments

UOAI Chat allows users to attach PDFs, Office files (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), text documents, and code files to provide context to the local AI.

  • On-Device Extraction: All file reading and text extraction (using PDFBox-Android and built-in UTF-8 readers) occurs 100% locally on your phone.
  • No Cloud Transmission: Neither your original files nor the extracted text are uploaded to any external server.
  • Scoping & Ephemeral Handling: Files are accessed only via the standard Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) system picker upon your direct user interaction. Once the message turn completes or the chat is cleared, attachment contents are discarded.

5. Third-Party Sharing, Telemetry & Advertising

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: There are no advertisements in UOAI Provider or UOAI Chat.
  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry SDKs: We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, Flurry, or any other tracking SDK.
  • Zero Crash Telemetry SDKs: We do not integrate third-party crash trackers (like Sentry or Crashlytics). Crash diagnostics stay within the standard Android OS bug report mechanisms.
  • Zero User Profiling: We do not build user profiles, track device identifiers (IMEI, Advertising ID, MAC address), or sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not collect, transmit, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because all processing is offline and on-device, our applications comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Because all data is stored exclusively in your device's private application sandbox:

  1. In-App Chat Clear: Tapping the "New Chat" icon in UOAI Chat immediately discards the current session and in-memory conversation history.
  2. Revoking Client Access: In UOAI Provider, you can revoke any approved client application at any time in the Clients tab.
  3. Full Data Wipe: You can wipe all downloaded models, credentials, tokens, and settings at any time by going to your phone's Settings → Apps → UOAI Provider (or UOAI Chat) → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  4. Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically and permanently purges all associated private storage from your device.

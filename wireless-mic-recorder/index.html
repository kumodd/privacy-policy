Wireless Mic Recorder Privacy Policy

Kumod (Clean Recorder Studio) built Wireless Mic Recorder (com.cleanrecorder.wirelessmic) as a privacy-first, local-network audio and video recording application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

100% Local Network • Mutual ECDH + AES-256-GCM • Zero Cloud Servers • Zero Ads

Wireless Mic Recorder requires no user accounts, runs no backend servers, includes zero third-party analytics or ad SDKs, and transmits peer-to-peer audio strictly over your local Wi-Fi, Mobile Hotspot, or Wi-Fi Direct connection with mutual 6-digit ECDH (P-256) key exchange and AES-256-GCM encryption.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or transmit any personal information, audio recordings, video recordings, contacts, location data, or usage analytics to our servers or any third party. The app requires no user account or sign-in and functions completely offline over your local Wi-Fi network or Mobile Hotspot.

2. Local Processing & Encryption

  • Audio & Video: All camera video, microphone voice, live DSP mixing, and optional Phone 2 internal device audio are processed exclusively on your devices.
  • Local Peer-to-Peer Transfer: Audio transmitted between your connected devices travels only across your local Wi-Fi, Hotspot, or Wi-Fi Direct network and is protected with mutual 6-digit ECDH (P-256) pairing and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • On-Device Storage: Recorded MP4 videos and lossless WAV safety backups remain privately inside the app's local storage on your device until you explicitly choose to export, share, or delete them.

3. Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

The app requests only the Android permissions necessary for its core features:

  • Camera: To record video in Video Recorder mode.
  • Microphone: To capture live speech on the Wireless Microphone device (and optional explicit fallback on the Recorder device).
  • Bluetooth & Nearby Wi-Fi Devices: Strictly to discover and pair your own nearby devices over local connections. We do not track or collect GPS or location data (neverForLocation on Android 12+).
  • Internal Audio Capture (MediaProjection): Requested only when you explicitly enable "Internal Phone Audio" on Phone 2 to stream backing tracks or media into Phone 1's Live Audio Mixer, and only after you approve Android's system consent prompt.
  • Notifications & Foreground Services: To display an active indicator while audio streaming or video recording is in progress.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

  • No Analytics or Advertising SDKs: The app contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or crash-reporting SDKs that exfiltrate user data.
  • Google Play Store Link: In the Free edition (1 wireless microphone), tapping the optional Pro upgrade prompt (com.cleanrecorder.wirelessmic.pro, up to 4 simultaneous wireless microphones) opens the Google Play Store app/website, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Local Storage, Data Retention & Deletion

All recordings and pairing keys are stored solely on your device (with allowBackup="false"):

  • Private In-App Recordings: filesDir/recordings/ (MP4 video takes) and filesDir/safety_audio/ (lossless WAV safety backups and live stereo mixdowns).
  • Local Preferences: Device display name, audio quality/video format/mixer UI style preferences, and locally stored trusted peer ECDH keys (SharedPreferences).
  • User-Initiated Exports: Copied to Movies/WirelessMicRecorder, Music/WirelessMicSafety, or a user-chosen folder via Android Storage Access Framework only when you tap Export or Share.

You have full control at all times:

  • Delete individual video takes or safety WAV backups directly inside the app.
  • Clear paired device keys anytime via Settings → Forget Paired Phones.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all private in-app recordings and settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Wireless Mic Recorder is a general-audience audio/video creation tool not directed to children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data from any users, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
